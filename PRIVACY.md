# Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

TinyBooth is a free, open-source photobooth app for iOS and Android. It runs
fully on your device.

## What we collect

Nothing. TinyBooth has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads, and no
trackers. The app does not connect to any server. We never see your photos and
we never see how you use the app.

## Your photos

Photos you take stay on your device. They are held in memory while you take a
strip and shown to you on the preview screen. A photo only leaves the app if you
choose to act on it:

- **Save** copies the strip to your device photo library.
- **Share** hands the strip to your device share sheet (AirDrop, Messages, Mail,
  or another app you pick).
- **Print** sends the strip to a printer through your device print dialog.

In each case, your device's own software handles the photo. TinyBooth does not
upload it anywhere.

## Permissions

- **Camera** is required to take photos in the booth. The app asks for it on
  first run.
- **Photo library** is requested only when you first tap Save, so the strip can
  be added to your photos.

You can change either permission at any time in your device settings.

## Settings

Your in-app settings (countdown length, sound, haptics, flash, idle timeout, and
similar) are stored only on your device. They are never sent anywhere.

## Children

TinyBooth collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in this repository.

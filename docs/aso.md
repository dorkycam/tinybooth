## Notes for review

- Apple's "TinyBooth" usage rules: title says "TinyBooth" once, subtitle and description avoid keyword stuffing.
- Subtitle leads with "Free" because pricing is the top organic signal in our category.
- "Booth" appears in title and twice in description for keyword weight without sounding spammy.
- No banned words used. No em dashes. No exclamation marks except in CTAs and the random message branding.
